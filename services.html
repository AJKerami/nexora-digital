Frequently asked questions

Before you start

What does a website cost?

One-page websites start at CAD $350, multi-page sites at CAD $500, and maintenance at CAD $50/month. The exact quote depends on the agreed scope.

Do I need to provide website content?

We'll discuss the text, photos, business details and service descriptions needed for your project. Any content creation or additional editing should be included in the quote.

Can you update an existing website?

Describe your current website and the changes you need. We can review the request and confirm whether it fits our services.

How long does a website take?

Timing depends on the number of pages, features, content availability and review process. We discuss a project schedule before work begins.
